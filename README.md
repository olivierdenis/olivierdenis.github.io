# Holographic Computational Universe

## Olivier Denis, 

Olivier Denis' research explores the fundamental informational foundations of the Universe through the yet published Holographic Computational Universe (HCU) framework, connecting quantum information, entropy, holography, irreversibility, spacetime, gravity within one conceptual architecture.

HCU proposes that physical reality emerges from informational organization and irreversible holographic records, offering a framework in which spacetime, temporal ordering, and gravitational phenomena are investigated as consequences of deeper quantum-informational and thermodynamic processes.
